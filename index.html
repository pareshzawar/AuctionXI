How it works

  1. The admin creates an auction and uploads the filled setup questionnaire: format, squad size, purse, captain and vice-captain prices, base price, increments, house rules.
  2. Upload the teams and players templates (mark each team's captain and vice-captain), check everything, then lock the player list. A new auction starts with a fresh upload.
  3. Share the auction code. Captains (and vice-captains, if allowed) enter their secret code to bid and see their purse and max bid; everyone else watches.
  4. Run the auction from the control room. Captains bid on their phones, or the auctioneer records paddle bids or just the final amount.
  5. Export the results and archive them in the repo.
