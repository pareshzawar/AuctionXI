# AuctionXI guide for organisers and captains

A 31-slide guide to every feature and how to use it, with screenshots of the app (all player names and photos are made-up sample data).

| File | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [AuctionXI-Guide.pdf](AuctionXI-Guide.pdf) | Read on a phone or laptop, or share on WhatsApp |
| [AuctionXI-Guide.pptx](AuctionXI-Guide.pptx) | Present it to your committee and captains (the slide text is in the speaker notes) |

**Part A, for organisers:** what you need, creating the auction, setup and rules, past seasons (team records and top performers), teams and team banners, players (auction, waiting list or registration cancelled), locking and small fixes after it, photos, privacy and sharing, the QR poster, the optional AI assistant (and what happens when it fails), the mock auction, the control room and its keys, searching players by name or number, the stage screen, SOLD with gavel and confetti, commentary with punchlines, the offline slides and Excel tracker, what to do if something goes wrong (offline pack, offline banner), the unsold round, results and next season's mini auction.

**Part B, for captains:** joining and bidding from the phone, the private PIN-locked workbook, **My plan** (targets, max bids and what each buy leaves), the *what if I buy…* calculator, notes on other teams, the scratch pad and Ask AI, and what everyone sees on their phones.

**Part C:** the auction-day checklist.
