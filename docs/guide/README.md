# AuctionXI guide for organisers and captains

A 25-slide guide to every feature and how to use it, with screenshots of the app (all player names and photos are made-up sample data).

| File | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [AuctionXI-Guide.pdf](AuctionXI-Guide.pdf) | Read on a phone or laptop, or share on WhatsApp |
| [AuctionXI-Guide.pptx](AuctionXI-Guide.pptx) | Present it to your committee and captains (the slide text is in the speaker notes) |

**Part A, for organisers:** what you need, creating the auction, setup and rules, teams, players (auction, waiting list or registration cancelled), photos, privacy and sharing, the optional AI assistant, the mock auction, the control room and its keys, searching players by name or number, the stage screen, the offline slides and Excel tracker, the unsold round, results and next season's mini auction.

**Part B, for captains:** joining and bidding from the phone, the private PIN-locked workbook, **My plan** (targets, max bids and what each buy leaves), the *what if I buy…* calculator, notes on other teams, the scratch pad and Ask AI, and what everyone sees on their phones.

**Part C:** the auction-day checklist.
